# RHFiles 0.1.36

## 中文

- 修正框选手势：按下后位移不足 4 像素仍算普通点击。因此现在在日期 / 类型 / 大小列或文件名右侧空白处**单击即可选中该行**（和资源管理器一致），只有真正拖动时才画框选择；在空白背景上单击也恢复为清除选择。
- 只有真正发生过框选拖动时，才会吞掉随后那次 click，普通点击永远正常传递。
- 新增两条框选回归测试，分别覆盖"空白处单击选中该行"和"拖动才画框并吞掉点击"。

## English

- Corrected the rubber-band gesture: a press that travels less than 4 pixels stays a plain click. Clicking the date/type/size cells or the blank space right of a name now selects that row (matching Explorer), and dragging is what draws the selection box. Clicking the empty background again clears the selection.
- Only a real marquee drag swallows the click that follows it; ordinary clicks always reach their handler.
- Added two rubber-band regressions covering "plain click on blank row space selects the row" and "only dragging draws a marquee and swallows the click".
