# RHFiles 0.1.35

## 中文

- 修复标签页悬停预览始终显示"0 个文件夹、0 个文件"：未访问过的后台标签页按需读取目录后再显示数量与前几项，并遵循"显示隐藏项目"开关。
- 回车打开与双击行为统一：两者现在走同一套逻辑（文件夹进入、ZIP 进入压缩包浏览、归档内条目解压、其他文件交给系统打开），Alt+↓ 也一致。
- 修复跨机器（UNC 网络位置与映射的网络驱动器）删除、移动报错：网络位置没有回收站，之前总是失败；现在网络路径自动改为直接删除，并在确认框里明确提示"网络位置将永久删除，无法还原"。
- 隐藏项目默认显示，并记住你的选择：工具栏按钮与设置中的"显示隐藏项目"开关互相同步、重启后保留。
- 右下角操作成功提示的停留时间可设置（2 / 5 / 10 秒或一直保留），默认 5 秒；选择"一直保留"时提示会带一个关闭按钮。
- 框选改进：在详情视图的日期 / 类型 / 大小列以及文件名右侧空白处按下即可拉框选择，图标与文件名上仍然是拖动文件；文件铺满时还可以用列表右侧的窄空白条起框。框选结束不再被随后的点击清空。

## English

- Fixed the tab hover preview always reporting "0 folders, 0 files": unvisited background tabs now load their directory on demand before showing counts and entries, and respect the show-hidden setting.
- Enter and double-click now share one open path: folders navigate, ZIPs open in the archive browser, entries inside archives extract, and other files open through the shell. Alt+Down matches too.
- Fixed delete and move failing on cross-machine paths (UNC shares and mapped network drives): network locations have no Recycle Bin, so recycling always failed. Network paths are now deleted directly, and the confirmation dialog states that they will be removed permanently.
- Hidden items are shown by default and the choice is remembered: the toolbar toggle and the new "Show hidden items" setting stay in sync across restarts.
- The corner success notices can now be configured to last 2 / 5 / 10 seconds or stay until dismissed, defaulting to 5 seconds; "keep until dismissed" adds a close button.
- Rubber-band selection improvements: dragging from the date/type/size cells or the blank space right of a file name now draws a selection box, while the icon and name still drag the file; when files fill the pane, the narrow strip at the right edge of the list always works. Releasing the drag no longer clears the selection it just made.
