# RHFiles Android 0.1.33

## 中文

1. 新增独立扫码页面：可从侧栏右上角三点菜单、侧栏“设置”下方、桌面长按 RHFiles 图标打开。
2. 自动识别设备组邀请、请求和批准信息。电脑生成一次性邀请后，手机扫码、核对并确认即可入组；普通二维码展示内容并提供“复制”，不自动打开网址或执行内容。
3. 移除 RHFiles 独立配对及旧连接，禁止旧密码凭据回退。普通 SMB、FTP、WebDAV 连接继续可用；配置同步不会恢复旧独立配对，也不会导入其他设备的组信任。
4. 明确提示“组公开信息”不是新手机的入组批准；保留离线请求/批准方式。邀请请求受次数和时间限制，写入结果不明时不盲目重放。
5. 旧独立连接的快捷访问一并清理；导入普通配置时保留本机有效设备组连接。

## English

1. Add a standalone QR reader in the drawer overflow menu, below Settings, and in the launcher long-press shortcut.
2. Recognize group invitations, requests and approvals. Confirm a computer's one-use invitation to join its group. Other QR contents are displayed with a Copy button; links never open automatically.
3. Remove independent RHFiles pairing, old profiles and password fallback. Ordinary SMB/FTP/WebDAV connections remain available. Configuration sync cannot resurrect old pairings or import group trust.
4. Distinguish public group data from approval for a new phone. Keep offline request/approval exchange and bounded, non-replayed invitation requests.
5. Clear retired connection bookmarks and preserve valid local group profiles when importing ordinary settings.
